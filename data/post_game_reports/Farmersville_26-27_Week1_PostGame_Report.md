## 💡 Coach Game-Plan Adjustments for Week 2

1. **Keep Driving `SLOT TRIG` & `TEXAS NASTY` as Base Identity:**
   - Accounted for **251 of 357 offensive yards (70%)** and **3 of 4 touchdowns**.
   - Defenses playing static Cover 3 cannot handle the vertical seams (`POST`, `WAVE`, `AXEL BRONCO`) paired with `ILLINOIS` inside.

2. **Clean Up 3rd-Down Protection & Cadence:**
   - 4 false starts/penalties and 3 sacks on 3rd down gave away manageable downs.
   - Replace slow-developing dropbacks (`CYBORG`) on 3rd & long with quick-rhythm concept passes or QB draws (`ILLINOIS Q`, `IRELAND Q`).

3. **Shelve Underperforming Looks:**
   - `SLOT DIP` (non-goal-line) and `FOX LT` generated negative yards on 50% of calls.
   - Move motion and shift packages into `TRIX` and `WEST` where spacing was cleaner.
